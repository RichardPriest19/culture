01

Understand Culture

Culture is largely invisible — it lives in assumptions, habits, and unspoken rules. These three models reveal what culture actually is, how it is structured, and which dimensions vary most across organisations and nations.

02

Classify Culture

Once you can see culture, you need to be able to name it. These four typology models give leaders a shared language to diagnose where their organisation sits — and what that means for leadership, strategy, and performance.

03

Change Culture

Understanding and classifying culture is not enough — leaders must be able to change it. These three models provide the practical frameworks for navigating cultural transformation without destroying what works.
